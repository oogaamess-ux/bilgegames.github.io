Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

BilgeGames develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our applications, why it is collected, and how it may be processed.

1. Information We Collect

BilgeGames does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.

Our applications and third-party services may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device type and device identifiers
  • Operating system and application version
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Application interactions and gameplay activity
  • Advertising interaction and performance data
  • Crash reports, diagnostics and technical data

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve our games
  • To display and measure advertisements
  • To understand application usage
  • To detect crashes, bugs and technical problems
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security issues
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

These services may collect and process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, application interactions and advertisement interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Personalized Advertising and Consent

Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, users may be shown a consent form before advertising-related information is processed. Users may also be able to review or change their privacy choices inside the application.

5. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers or similar personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary for advertising, analytics, diagnostics, fraud prevention, security or legal compliance.

BilgeGames does not sell users’ directly identifiable personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to their own retention policies.

You may reset or delete your advertising identifier through your device settings. You may also contact us regarding data directly controlled by BilgeGames.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our applications, third-party services or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page together with a revised update date.
